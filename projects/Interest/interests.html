Music

Hero Interests

Music has always been something I really loved and used every day. It can determine your mood for the day, it can change your mood, or bring lots of people together. Whether I’m listening to different songs from an album of a new artist I’ve never heard from before, replaying new favorite songs, or trying to isolate every instrument of a song and try to hear each one on a track, I find myself always interested by how sound can give people complex emotions that words can’t capture.

Being part of a band taught me the value of learning to listen just as carefully as I played, and trying to use my own instincts with music to the energy of people around me. There is a lot of adrenaline when rehearsing a new song, practicing it, and then having the rush of performing it live on stage, gave me a great appreciation for the dedication it takes to musicians to make art.
