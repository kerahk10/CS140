Jon Bernthal

Hero Interests

Jon Bernthal is one of my favorite actors because in my own opinion, he brings a raw authenticity to all of the characters he acts as. He shows real resillience in some of is characters, and the realness of human pain. He can capture the weight of trauma that has allowed him to build a great connection with audiences.

The Walking Dead
One of my all time favorite shows to watch was the walking dead. He played Shane Walsh, a sheriff's deputy who was protective for his group and led to his moral descent.
The Odyssey
In this movie he was the betrayed king. He brought a lot of grit to a cast that with Matt Damon and Tom Holland. He also said his popular phrase "Let me tell you something" from The Walking Dead, which was put into the dialogue.
Spider-Man: Brand New Day
Jon plays as Frank Castle from The Punisher, crossing paths with Peter Parker to serve as a protector and a cautionary character. I enjoyed his acting in this movie because he had great comedic timing that contrasted well with his dark persona.
