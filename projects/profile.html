Hello, my name is Kerah Kahuena

I was born and raised on Oahu, raised by my parents Christine and Darroll Kahuena. I graduated from Kahuku High School in 2026, where I spent my time doing both music and sports. For 5 years, played percussion in the school band, including a year on the bass drum for marching band and 5 years for Pep band. I also spent two years competing on the cross country team.

Music has always been a massive part of my life, it's been an interest of mine since elementary school and playing in a band in high school. Even though I enjoy exploring almost every genre, my favorite artists are Kaytranada, Tyla, jamiroquai, and PinkPanthress. I have a lot more artists that I enjoy, but these are my top four. Music has always been such an influential part of my life.
